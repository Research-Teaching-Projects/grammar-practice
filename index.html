    The rules

    The five patterns to fix first

    • Subject and verb agreement. Plural subjects take verbs without -s (all of us want, teachers work). Singular subjects take -s (he does, it has).
    • A or an. Use an before a vowel sound (an example, an employee, an important role).
    • Missing "the" and missing plurals. Say the 1950s, the human race, and one of the + plural noun.
    • Run-on sentences and commas. Do not join two full sentences with a comma. Put a comma after opening phrases such as In this essay,.
    • Confused words. than/then, their/there, its/it's, sometimes/sometime, and the spelling of because.
